To support the launch of Dune: Prophecy, Max teamed up with Discord to launch the first ever Video Quest to drive awareness and engagement for the TV series.

Results

85%
Video completion rate
100%
Reward redemption rate
Objective

Max aimed to build excitement and drive awareness for its highly anticipated TV series Dune: Prophecy, engaging audiences and creating buzz ahead of the premiere.

Solution

Max partnered with Discord to launch the first-ever Video Quest, featuring a 2:38-minute trailer that captivated users and achieved exceptional engagement.

Users who completed the Quest unlocked an exclusive Dune: Prophecy avatar decoration inspired by an iconic series landmark, sparking excitement and generating earned media as fans showcased their rewards.

Results
  • Reached an 85% VCR, highlighting exceptional audience interest and attention.
  • Rewards claimed at a 100% redemption rate.
  • Generated significant earned impressions and click-through engagement, amplifying visibility and campaign impact.
