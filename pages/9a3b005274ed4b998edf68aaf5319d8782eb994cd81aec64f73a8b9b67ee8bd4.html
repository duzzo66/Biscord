Discord @ GDC 2025

Discord is back and ready for yet another incredible Game Developers Conference 2025. Find us at the Discord booth on the Expo Floor in the South Hall (#S1627).

See Programming Schedule

GDC 2025 Programming Schedule

Whether it’s your first time at Moscone Center or your 10th, we’ve got lots of events to help inspire and kickstart your game-building experience on Discord.

A Game Developer's Guide: Building and Running Your Discord Community

March 19, 2025 11:00 AM - 11:30 AM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Colin Loretz, Senior Developer Advocate, Discord

‍

Learn how to leverage Discord as a powerful tool for early-stage game development and community building. This talk provides practical insights into creating and managing private playtest servers that foster meaningful relationships with your earliest supporters while gathering crucial gameplay feedback.

Discord x Wicked Saints: The Power of Gaming for Well-Being

March 19, 2025 11:45 AM - 12:15 PM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Savannah Badalich, Senior Director of Policy, Discord; Patricia Noel, Mental Health Policy Manager, Discord; Jessica Murrey, CEO + Co-Founder, Wicked Saints

‍

Gaming has always been about more than just play—it’s a space for connection, learning, and personal expression. According to eMarketer, Gen Z is increasingly turning to gaming as a social space for authentic interactions. As they do so, platforms like Discord and games like World Reborn are working to support their well-being. Join leaders from Discord and Wicked Saints as they explore how gaming fosters meaningful relationships, supports mental health, and can empower players to take real-world action.

Farm Merge Valley: How CoolGames Maximized Game-Platform Fit on Discord

March 19, 2025 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM PST
West Hall 2011
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Ann-Marie Harbour, Director of Game Development, Discord; Laurens Rutten, CEO, CoolGames

‍

Discover how CoolGames successfully launched Farm Merge Valley on Discord Activities, blending the merge-3 puzzle genre with farming simulation to captivate millions of players. This talk explores how the team optimized the game for Discord's inherently social environment, driving daily player engagement and fostering a vibrant community. Learn key strategies behind their adaptation process and how they built a Discord community server that grew to over 250,000 members in record time.

Deep Dive with Supervive: Theorycraft's Integration with the Discord Social SDK

March 19, 2025 2:15 PM - 2:45 PM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Andrew Johnston, Staff Software Engineer, Theorycraft Games

‍

Social play is core to SUPERVIVE, and integrating Discord’s Social SDK helped us improve the way players connect, communicate, and play together. In this talk, we’ll walk through how we integrated the Social SDK, extended the existing social graph with Discord friends, enabled seamless activity invites into our party system with Discord rich presence, and ensured all players, regardless of Discord linking status, could access the features.

Insights for Devs: Engage and Grow Your Audience on Discord

March 19, 2025 3:30 PM - 4:30 PM PST
South Hall, Industry Stage
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Tim Liu, Staff Data Scientist, Discord; Robinson Eaton, Staff User Experience Researcher, Discord

‍

Join Discord's data and UXR teams as we dive into platform insights and developer success stories that reveal what Discord's gaming audience truly cares about. Through real examples, we'll show how games of all types have built thriving communities by understanding their players and leveraging Discord's unique social features. Learn practical strategies from developers who've successfully built and fostered engaged communities, and discover how you can apply these lessons to your own game and community.

Discord For Game Devs: Build Where The World Plays

March 20, 2025 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM PST
West Hall 2020/2022
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Jason Citron, CEO and Founder, Discord; Tom Jacques, VP of Engineering, Discord; Emory Irpan, Group Product Marketing Manager, Discord; Sally Huang, Senior Director of Product, Discord

‍

With a thriving community of 200 million MAU spending over 1.5+ billion hours gaming each month, the heartbeat of games lives on Discord. Join us for a look at the latest and greatest on all the ways we are investing in and enabling game developers to tap into the power of the platform to fuel the growth of their game.

Learn with Little Umbrella: Building and Scaling an AI Game on Discord

March 20, 2025 12:15 PM - 12:45 PM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Timothy Johnson, CTO, Little Umbrella

‍

Discover how Little Umbrella leveraged Discord's robust developer tools and large, active user base to build and scale their AI-driven multiplayer game. Learn about their integration journey, technical challenges, and the impressive milestones they achieved, making their social game accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

Powering Player Connections: How Discord's Social SDK Boosts Your Game

March 20, 2025 1:15 PM - 1:45 PM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Scott Williams, Senior Engineering Manager, Discord; Tessa Siegel, Senior Software Engineer, Discord

‍

Friends make gaming better. Discord is the place where friends talk before, during, and after gaming. Come and see how our brand new Social SDK brings some of that Discord magic right into your games. Your users can bring their friends list with them, invite them into the game, and talk with them via text or voice. Join us to learn how to leverage this SDK and give your players more ways to connect with their friends. And yes, there will be memes.

From Vision to Vive'n: Supervive's Discord-Driven Development Strategy

March 20, 2025 2:00 PM → 3:00 PM PST
West Hall 2020/2022
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Sally Huang, Senior Director of Product, Discord; Jon Belliss, Executive Producer, Theorycraft Games

‍

Join Theorycraft Games executive producer Jon Belliss as he talks through the entire development journey of SUPERVIVE, including choosing to develop in the open with a playtesting community since just a few months in; why Theorycraft chose Discord as their primary community development platform; and how they use Discord to power their development, feedback, and community strategy.

Fuel Your Player Retention: Leveraging Notifications for Activities

March 20, 2025 3:30 PM → 4:00 PM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627
Show more
Show less

Presented by: Preston Hulcy, Senior Software Engineer - Activities, Discord

‍

Join us to explore how Discord's Bot DM notifications can revolutionize your game's retention strategy. Learn how interactive notifications enable in-game actions and direct gameplay access through deep-linked messages. With partners seeing 2-10x retention improvements, we'll share implementation strategies, best practices, and key guidelines for API usage and compliance. Learn to create engaging notifications that drive player retention.

Building Your First Discord Activity with Mojiworks

March 21, 2025 11:00 AM → 11:30 AM PST
Discord Booth, South Hall #S1627

‍

Learn how Mojiworks leveraged Discord's Embedded App SDK to create immersive and engaging social gaming experiences. Learn about their journey, the impact of their game 'Chef Showdown', and get insights and advice for developers looking to build unique Activities on Discord.
