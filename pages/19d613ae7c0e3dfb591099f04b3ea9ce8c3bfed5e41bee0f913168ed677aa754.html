Crisis Text Line

US-based users can connect with a live volunteer Crisis Counselor at Crisis Text Line: a free, 24/7, confidential text-based mental health support service in both English and Spanish.

Text DISCORD to 741741

With a directory of crisis support in over 100+ countries, users can find free, confidential support in their country via chat, text, or phone.

Wellbeing & Empowerment Principles

We believe digital spaces can be sources of connection, growth, and belonging for everyone on Discord. With Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab, we've established four principles that guide our mission across the breadth of our work.

Gaming Fosters Meaningful Connection
We recognize that gaming and online communities foster transferable life skills and meaningful social connection. We understand virtual interactions support mental wellbeing and healthy emotional regulation. We inform design for authentic relationship-building that recognizes different social preferences, while understanding gaming culture challenges and working with internal teams to address them.
Championing User Agency & Transparency
Through research, partnerships, and user voice, we inform policies, programs, and resources that respect user agency. We balance autonomy and safety, reflecting developmental needs and cultural contexts. We maintain transparent communication about our decisions and their impact, creating pathways for intergenerational understanding that support guardian-teen relationships.
Mental Health & Digital Wellness
We recognize mental health support and digital literacy as fundamental to thriving communities where users engage authentically and sustainably. We understand that gaming can serve as stress relief and emotional regulation, and that digital spaces can meaningfully support mental wellbeing and meaningful connections. We aim to increase access to mental health resources, providing users with concrete tools for managing wellbeing in online spaces.
Wellbeing by Design
We provide expert guidance on policies that support gaming and community experience while preserving positive dynamics. We prioritize solutions delivering tangible safety improvements with high user engagement, making conscious trade-offs that preserve what makes Discord special. We take a culturally competent approach, recognizing that wellbeing and empowerment differ across regions.
