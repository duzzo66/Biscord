Frequently Asked Questions

What countries are Discord gift cards available in?

Discord gift cards are available in the US and United Kingdom, along with select retailers in Canada, France, Italy, and Spain. We’re always exploring new places we could bring gift cards in the future, stay tuned!

Where can I buy a Discord gift card?

You can buy Discord gift cards at select retailers depending on your country or region. If you're in the US, check our website here. If you're in the rest of the world, head to our Help Center.

Can I use my gift card balance on mobile?

You can view and use your gift card balance in the desktop app or on a web browser, but not within the mobile app. To redeem a gift card on mobile, head to discord.com/redeem. To spend your balance on mobile, head to discord.com/login in a browser. Anything you buy will show up across all your devices.

Can I redeem a gift card from another country?

Gift cards can only be redeemed in the country they were purchased in. To redeem, your account's Country/Region setting must match the gift card's currency. One exception: gift cards priced in euros can be redeemed in any country that uses euros. Make sure to check which countries Discord gift cards are available in before purchasing.

Why isn't my gift card code working?

Let’s help with that! Here are some pro tips and considerations:

  • Typo? Make sure you're entering the code exactly as shown on the back of the card.
  • Wrong Country? Gift cards can only be redeemed in the country they were purchased in. If your Country/Region doesn't match the card's currency, redemption will be blocked. You can see what region your account is set to under Country/Region in User Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.
  • Balance or redemption limit reached. Your account may have reached its wallet balance or redemption limit.
  • Platform pro tip. Just a heads up, you can only view and spend your balance on the desktop app or web browser. Items purchased show up wherever you use Discord.

If none of the above apply and your code still isn't working, please contact Discord support with your card details and a photo of the back of the card.
