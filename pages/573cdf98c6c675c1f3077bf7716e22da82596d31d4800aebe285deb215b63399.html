Work at Discord

Our employees aren’t just the most talented folks in the industry (we may be a little biased), they’re also deeply passionate about gaming, fostering meaningful connections, and of course, Discord.

See All Jobs

Be a part of the future of gaming

We believe Discord is uniquely positioned to shape the future of gaming. We aren’t just imagining what it may look like, we’re building Discord to be the perfect place to talk while playing games on any platform, no matter what device you play on.

Work with people just as passionate as you

Much like the people on Discord, our employees can’t be put in a box (but maybe in a server). We come from all walks of life, and with our own unique perspectives, but we all care about the same thing: making Discord your home while gaming.

Where every idea is heard and valued

We don't just build Discord, we use it too! All of us want to help make Discord the best place to play games with friends, and everyone's input matters.

Don’t just imagine the future of gaming — build it with us.

Questions?

Questions about our hiring process? Check out our frequently asked questions.

What states do you hire in? Do I have to live in San Francisco to work for Discord?

While we have no requirement to work from the office, we’re hiring most roles in the SF Bay Area to foster a vibrant central hub in the region. For most roles, candidates will need to reside in or be willing to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties).

Not every role requires candidates to be based in the SF Bay Area. In some cases, we continue to hire from across the U.S. Your recruiter can share more about location expectations for the role you’re interested in.

Where are your offices located?

We have one office in San Francisco and one in Amsterdam.

What countries do you hire in?

The majority of our roles are US-based. We are able to hire full-time employees in the US, Canada (only Ontario and British Columbia), and the Netherlands. Other roles are engaged in international locations through our hiring partner, Pebl, which serves as the Employer of Record. Each job posting will state the location requirement, so be sure to review all the details in the description.

Does Discord sponsor employees?

Discord’s immigration policy includes:

  • Continued sponsorship for any candidate with a current O-1, TN, E-3, H-1B1, or H-1B visa, provided they are eligible
  • Sponsorship of new TN or E-3 visas for Mexican/Canadian or Australian candidates
  • Sponsorship of Green Card for employees after 6 months of employment
  • Continued sponsorship of the Green Card process after 3 months of employment for candidates who have already received their priority date

If you have any questions about these policies, be sure to connect with your recruiter should you be selected to interview!

I submitted an application, when will I hear back from a recruiter?

Thanks a ton for applying! We do our best to respond to each person who applies within two weeks. Given the incredibly high number of applicants to some roles, we cannot guarantee each candidate will get a response directly from a recruiter.

I’m interested in a role at Discord, can I speak to a recruiter or hiring manager about it?

We’re hyped you’re interested! Based on the volume of applications Discord receives, recruiters and hiring managers are unable to meet with everyone who’s interested. The best thing to do is apply for the role and the team will be sure to review your application.

Can I apply to multiple positions at a time?

You can! Although, we recommend that you apply only to the roles you are most interested in and qualified for. Please note that you may hear back at different times for each role you apply to, and you can expect a separate update for each role.

I saw a job posting I was interested in, but I no longer see it on the careers page. What does this mean?

This could mean a variety of things - ranging from high applicant volume that needs to be reviewed, to the role already being filled. Keep an eye on the jobs page for any similar roles, or the role being posted again!

What do I need to know before my interview?

If you’re selected to interview, your recruiter will share all the details you need to know about the entire interview process - including how to prepare for each stage. Don’t hesitate to ask questions!

Do I have to write a Cover Letter?

Double check the job description to see if one is required! It’s never a bad idea to write a cover letter, since it gives us a chance to get to know you better.

What if I get an error when trying to apply?

Applicants sometimes run into one of these errors:

  • 500 Internal Server Error
  • “Well this is awkward. There was a problem submitting your application. Please try again or hit up our team at jobs@discordapp.com”

If you see one of these, we recommend these solutions:

  • Try accessing the application by using an alternate browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or Opera.
  • Use an incognito browser tab to see if that resolves the issue.

If you run into an error regarding uploading an html or word doc, try uploading a PDF instead!

If I’ve been referred, do I also need to formally apply?

Nope! You do not need to apply if someone has already referred you. When someone refers you, your referrer submits your resume and any other application materials on your behalf. From there, we’ll be in touch if you are a good fit for the role.
